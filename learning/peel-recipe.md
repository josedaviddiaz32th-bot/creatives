## Owner corrections after v3 (Nov 8, evening)
- **No pauses.** Resistance means continuous, slow, even motion with stretch and skin lift; the release line never stops, not even for a second.
- **Hands lit like the face.** Both hands keep the same light and the same skin tone as her face in every frame; a hand never goes darker as it nears the lens or moves into shadow. Light the scene so both hands are in the same light (no lamp on one side only for shots with hands).

## Why it works (what we learned the hard way)
1. **Every model gets the first half of a peel right and breaks on the last third.** Tested: Kling 3.0 pro, Kling 4.0 and Gemini Veo ×2. They let the rest go at once, slide it over the eyes, or ball it in a fist. **So never ask one clip for the whole peel. Chain two clips:** clip A peels the first half; clip B starts from clip A's last good frame and peels the rest. The seam is invisible because it's the same frame.
2. **Start mid-peel** (a still with the end already lifted and pinched). The model never has to invent the first lift.
3. **Pull outward, away from the forehead and a little sideways,** in one smooth, continuous pull that never pauses. Not a fold-back across the face, and not "low and close to the skin".
4. **Anchor hand:** fingertips press the temple flat against the pull.
5. **Resistance** (the owner's "it's actually stuck"): the release line creeps at one slow pace; the stuck part never slides; the skin lifts a hair and springs back at the release line; the peeled part ripples with moving streaks of light.
6. **No end frame on the peel clips.** An end pose pulled Kling 4.0 into a swoop across her face.
7. **End the shot the moment the patch comes free.** Every take dangled the free patch across the face right after, so cut to the next beat there.
8. **Gemini (Veo) first:** it's free and it gave the best stretch and resistance. Use Higgsfield Kling 3.0 std (6 cr for 4 s) for clip B or when Gemini is out.
9. Prompt review still runs on every prompt (owner rule), and every take gets a 5–10 fps frame check.
