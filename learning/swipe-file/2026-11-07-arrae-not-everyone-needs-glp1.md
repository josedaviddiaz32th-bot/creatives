## Core mechanism
**Contrarian permission from a peer:** "you don't need the big intervention,
sometimes you just need a smaller, gentler thing", which relieves the guilt
and fear of the viewer who has been avoiding the heavy option, then the
product arrives as the peer's own gentle pick.

## Mapping notes (for reuse)
Fits any category with a feared "big" option: Botox/needles (our P1, P4),
surgery, prescription drugs. Keep it respectful: permission, not a knock on
the big option.
