## Core mechanism
**Show the gap on one face at once:** a single split frame makes the viewer see the distance between
her filtered self and her real self, which is exactly the "I filter and feel like a fraud" feeling;
the honest reveal plus reassurance earns likes, saves and shares.

## Mapping notes (for reuse)
A cheap, code-made device for any "the photo isn't the real me" problem: a split wipe over one still
("FILTER | ME"), then a closing callback with the same split ("LAST NIGHT | THIS MORNING") as the
like-for-like before/after. Never copy her face, words, caption or statistic. Works best paired with
a cause-reveal talking head (see `2026-11-07-nhilan-ugly-in-photos-camera-reveal.md`).
