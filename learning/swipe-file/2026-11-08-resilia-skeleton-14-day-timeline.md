## Core mechanism
**"Watch it work inside you, day by day":** a cute see-through body acts out a dated timeline of small, relatable wins (Day 1… Day 14) while macro CGI shows the hidden mechanism, so the viewer rehearses their own transformation and believes the cause before the product is ever pitched.

## Funnel stage and awareness, and why
TOF→MOF. It is built for cold, older, symptom-aware viewers who don't know a cause (fatigue, swelling, sleep, BP): the first 95 s educate and reframe rather than sell, the brand name arrives late, and the CTA is "Learn more" to an advertorial-style landing. The category word and the pack in frame from second 0 filter for people already curious about natural heart support, so it also works as MOF for solution-aware viewers. It is not BOF: no price, no reviews, no comparison.

## Mapping notes (for reuse)
Transferable shell: (1) result-in-timeframe hook, (2) "Day N" ladder of small sensory/visible wins, (3) inside-the-body mechanism visual, (4) "you thought it was age" reframe, (5) product origin story, (6) guarantee + scarcity + emotional close. For skincare/wrinkle patches: a stylised face/skin cross-section over 14 nights is the analogue.
