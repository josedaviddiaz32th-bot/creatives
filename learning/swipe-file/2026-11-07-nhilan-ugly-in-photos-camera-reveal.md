## Core mechanism
**"It's not your face, it's a hidden cause"**: a peer names a private insecurity, moves the
blame from the viewer's face to a specific cause she didn't know about, and *demonstrates* the
cause on camera. The relief plus the "wait, really?" fact is what earns saves, shares and
"I always thought…" comments.

## Mapping notes (for reuse)
Fits any problem the viewer blames on herself when the real cause is outside her control or
fixable: looking angry/tired in photos (lines pressed in overnight), hair breakage (pillowcase),
bloating, posture. For us: TOF-U. The reference has no product, so our version must add the
last-~20% answer and the brand (strategy §2); keep the demo beat, it's the strongest moment.
