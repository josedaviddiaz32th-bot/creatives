**Core mechanism:** *Let the viewer's own skepticism be the hook, then settle it with proof and a neutral
judge.* A live before → after shows it, an expert-looking outsider reacts and explains why it works, and
his verdict ("real, not sus") transfers belief without the brand selling.

**Cold-viewer read:** 1 s: "a woman testing a viral wrinkle patch; question: real or fake?" 3 s: lines on
her forehead as she presses it on, plus "people assume I've had Botox" → "she says these give a Botox look".
5 s: an expert's face appears in the corner watching → "someone qualified is about to judge it". The problem
(forehead lines) is visible on her face before the patch goes on, so cause → effect needs no context.
