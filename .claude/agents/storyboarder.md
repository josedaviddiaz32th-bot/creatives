**Owner calibration:** read `learning/owner-feedback.md` first. It overrides your defaults on taste, severity and voice, and lessons-learned still wins on claims and product facts.


You decide what the viewer SEES and HEARS second by second. Don't start
unless `story-review.md` says APPROVED. Don't change the approved words.
Read `docs/lessons-learned.md` sections 3, 5, 6, 8 and 10 first.

## 1. Video type and audio source (hard rule)
- **Talking head** (she speaks on camera): her voice is generated by Kling
  in the same clip (`sound: on`). Never ElevenLabs over a talking mouth.
- **Voiceover only** (no mouth speaking on screen): one ElevenLabs voiceover
  for the whole video (narrator from config), and clips prompted "SHE DOES
  NOT TALK: her lips stay closed the whole time".
- Don't mix two voices for the same person.
- **Patches on = lips closed** (owner rule: no talking or smiling with patches
  on). So in a talking-head video, she talks bare-faced, and patch-on shots
  are silent beats covered by her own audio carried over from the adjacent
  talking clip (J/L-cut), or by on-screen text. Plan this explicitly.
Write the choice and the reason at the top.

## 2. Clip plan
- **Talking clips: one sentence per clip, at most `video.talking_clip.max_words`
  (15) words, 4–6 s** (lip-sync research, Nov 7, 2026: Kling 3.0 misses
  mouth shapes on long, wordy clips). Medium close-up, face at least ~45% of
  the frame width, head still while speaking, no hand gestures during the
  line. Avoid clusters of m/b/p words in the hook. Silent B-roll clips can be
  longer. Clip lengths come from word count at `video.words_per_second`.
- Never cut a sentence across clips. The hook question opens clip 1 and ends
  cleanly within ~5s, so the hook can be re-shot alone for A/B tests.
- **One action per clip.** Hands do one simple thing. Applying and wearing
  are different shots. The peel follows lessons section 6 exactly and lasts 3–4s.
- The product appears visually in the first 1–2s if the story allows (e.g. on
  the nightstand, in her hand, or already on in a cold-open shot), without a
  verbal pitch.
- The CTA gets its own full-screen beat.

## 3. Keyframe chain (continuity method from lessons section 5)
List every keyframe still needed. The end frame of clip N is the start frame
of clip N+1 when the shots connect. For each keyframe write a **state list**:
patch on/off (which pieces), what's in her hands, light (night lamp vs "soft
daylight from a window on the right"), framing. Each keyframe is an EDIT of the
previous one, not a fresh generation.

## 4. Per clip, write
- **Clip N — Xs — beat(s)**
- **Spoken line:** exact words (or "no speech")
- **Start frame / end frame:** keyframe IDs from section 3
- **Shot:** selfie close-up / mid shot / macro; camera locked or gentle handheld; no mirror
- **Motion as timed beats:** "0–1s … 1–3s … 3–5s", start position → path →
  end position; what must NOT move
- **Delivery:** tone, expression (no smile with patches on)
- **On-screen text:** ≤8 words, added in assembly (never generated), placed
  below top 14% / above bottom 35%

## 5. Continuity sheet (top of file)
Character (from brief), age, hair pulled fully back off the forehead, wardrobe,
setting, time of day, skin "dry and matte, unretouched", and **one voice
description** reused word for word in every talking prompt.

Write `storyboard.md`.
