# Story — run 2026-11-07-1926 — slot 1-T (P1, TOF-U) — v3 (version under review, last allowed revision)

## 1. Concept
**"Laughing in real life, mad in the photo."** P1 sees herself looking angry in a group photo
from a night she loved, then finds out the cause isn't her mood or her age: a lot of those lines
get pressed into her pillow every night. It fits P1 because her desire is being liked in photos (Avatars
angle 1: "Avoiding group pictures"), she's never connected those lines to anything she can fix
(TOF-U), and the reveal is a fact she'll want to tell a friend.

## 2. Format
Mixed: a silent POV group-photo moment (frame one), then one peer talking head on her bed the same
night (native Kling voice), with two silent cutaways in code (tonight's photo, her asleep), and a
morning result. Structure from the swipe
`learning/swipe-file/2026-11-07-nhilan-ugly-in-photos-camera-reveal.md`: private insecurity →
withheld cause → visual of the cause → the answer.

## 5. The turn, explained
She has just found out the lines are pressed into her pillow every night, so the answer is something that
holds the skin flat while she sleeps; "So tonight" makes it her first night trying it, which is why
tonight's photo still shows the problem. The box has been beside her since 0:01.5 and her face is already
washed for bed, so holding up a piece is a reach, not a pivot.
