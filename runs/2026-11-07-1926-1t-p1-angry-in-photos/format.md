## Plan for the scriptwriter
- **Type:** POV group-photo moment → peer talking head with a demo → patch as the answer → morning result.
- **Hook pattern:** question callout of her moment (the hook direction), with motion in frame one (friends
  squeezing in, timer blink, flash). ≤8-word on-screen text. Draft 10+.
- **Pacing:** reference is one take; ours cuts more (party POV → bedroom talking head → silent apply →
  morning). Keep the talking head long and unbroken through problem + reveal; one action per shot.
- **Shot style:** front-camera selfie, phone framing, natural/lamp light, unretouched skin.
- **Who speaks:** only her, on camera (native Kling voice). Friends never speak (laughter/room sound only).
  Patches-on beats are silent, lips closed, no smile.
- **Product timing:** box in frame from the first bedroom shot without comment; spoken from ~the last 20–25%.
- **Proof:** the demo + a before/after (last night's photo vs. this morning; overlay in code).
