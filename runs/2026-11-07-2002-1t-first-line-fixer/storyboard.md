## Video type and audio source

**Talking head (native voice) with silent code cutaways.** She speaks on camera, so her voice is
generated by Kling 3.0 in the same clip (`sound: on`) in three clips (A, B1, B2). **No ElevenLabs
job.** One voice for her, never mixed.

- **Patches on = lips closed:** she talks bare-faced only. The one patch-on frame (K4 press-on) is
  a **silent still** with code motion, covered by her voice carried over from clip B2 underneath
  (L-cut). We never cut back to B2 video after the press-on still (review note c): B2 video is only
  on screen 0:30.8–0:35.8, and her face is bare throughout B2.
- Cutaways (POV group photo, FILTER | ME split, sleeping, press-on, YESTERDAY | TODAY card) are
  checked Nano Banana 2 stills with code motion, not Kling clips. Every second her voice is heard
  is paid Kling footage, including under cutaways.
- **All on-screen text and brand names are added in code** (never generated), ≤8 words per
  element, below the top 14% (y ≥ 269 px) and above the bottom 35% (y ≤ 1248 px) of 1080 × 1920.
  Per review note b, the story's overlays are the **only** text on screen (no auto-captions on
  top), and in the split window they sit clear of her eyes and brow.
- SFX (code, from a commercial-use library): two timer beeps + shutter at 0:00–0:01.2, soft
  pillow rustle at 0:27.8. No music under speech (or a commercial-library bed ducked ~10 dB).
  Master −16 LUFS.
