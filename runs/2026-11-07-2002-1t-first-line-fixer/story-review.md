### The scriptwriter's two judgment calls

- **Fix 6 on screen instead of spoken: accepted.** Speaking "It ages me more than I'd like." (7 words,
  3.04s) would push clip A from 14s to about 17s. That's over the 15s clip max, so it would need a
  split and about 3 more billed seconds (≈7.5 credits), taking the first pass to about 121.5–124.5,
  over 120. My math agrees. On screen, it's her exact quote at the most emotional frame (the
  FILTER | ME split), and sound-off viewers, who are most viewers, get it at full strength. The
  trade-off: sound-on viewers read one line while hearing another ("I filter it, feel like a
  fraud" under "And it keeps getting worse."). That's normal for TikTok, but see storyboard note b.
- **Fix 5's sixth still: accepted.** It's what makes the before/after believable to a woman who
  calls over-edited before/afters "can't be real". Making YESTERDAY as an edit of TODAY ("CHANGES
  ONLY") is the right method (lessons §4: edit, don't regenerate) and keeps light and framing
  identical. Cost is +1.5–2 credits, still under 120. The price is a thin margin: 3–6 credits,
  which is two still redos at most. Watch the edit itself: it *adds* lines back to the TODAY face.
  They must match the identity frame's lines exactly, not deeper. A YESTERDAY that looks worse
  than her real face would be the "rigged" before/after we're trying to avoid.
