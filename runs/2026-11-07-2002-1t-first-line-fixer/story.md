## 1. Concept
**"Can't take a group photo without filtering your lines?"** A 27-year-old filters every group
photo because of her frown lines, forehead lines and the crinkling by her eyes, then feels like a
fraud. Her friends look smooth; it ages her more than she'd like, and it keeps getting worse. She's
researched tretinoin, vitamin C, peptides, even "Botox of lesser strength", but she's scared of
looking unnatural. Then the self-check: ever notice your lines look worst right when you wake up?
A lot of them get pressed in every night. So now she sleeps in The Wrinkle Smooth Face Set, which
holds her skin flat all night, and the video ends on a like-for-like split, yesterday vs today,
same light, no filter.

**Why it fits her:** it's built from her own quotes ("can't take any without using a filter
anymore but then I just feel like a fraud", "crinkling by my eyes", "it ages me more than I'd
like", "they continue to get worse and worse", "Botox of lesser strength"), her comparison to
smoother-skinned peers, her frown lines and her ingredient research. The cause (lines pressed in
every night) is new to her (TOF-U) and points to something she can do tonight without needles.

## 2. Format
Mixed: peer talking head (native Kling voice, her bedroom at night) with code cutaways made from
checked stills: the group-photo POV, a "FILTER | ME" split of that photo (device from the Lizzy

forehead piece flat, lips closed), and a like-for-like "YESTERDAY | TODAY" morning split under the
CTA. Base structure: the Nhi Lan cause-reveal (`format.md`).

part of her room, not a pitch.

## 6b. Hypothesis
Tests whether the **First-Line Fixer (27, filters her lines out of every photo) × TOF-U ×
POV/cause-reveal with a "FILTER | ME" split hook** earns attention from people who weren't looking
for patches. One variable vs other TOF slots: persona + her filter trigger. Decided by **engagement
rate** (likes + comments + shares + saves ÷ views) and **hook rate** (2s views ÷ views) vs the TOF
median (WIN ≥ 1.5×), plus PHOTO keyword comments, "yes"-type replies to the wake-up self-check, and
whether the audience skews women 25–34.
