# Story: "REAL or SUS?" expert stitch, P4 (run 2026-11-08-1338)

Stage 3 (scriptwriter). Reads `brief.md`, `format.md`, lessons (§0–3), owner feedback, config. No credits.

## 1. Concept
A P4 woman's own night-and-morning clip (patch on, patch off, smoother forehead) gets stitched by a calm,
expert-looking stranger who asks the question she's asking herself, "real or sus?", explains why it works in
plain words, why this kit beats tape, and calls it: real. **Why P4:** she wants forehead results "without
Botox or filler" and finds every alternative "a mixed bag"; a neutral verdict on a live demo is the help she
needs to choose.

## 2. Format
Mixed: silent UGC (0–12 s, music/room tone) + talking expert (native voice) 12–29.5 s + end card. From
`format.md`. All video.

## 3. Hook
- **Main (on screen, 0–12 s):** "REAL or SUS? Wrinkle smoothing patches" over her holding the clear band up
  to the lens. It's the reference's own question, kept by the owner's "same on-screen text pattern".
- Alternatives drafted (MOF: about choosing / doubting patches; for hook tests later, same body):

it work. The "choosing" line answers the question a P4 viewer asks next ("couldn't I just use tape?") in the
expert's neutral voice.

## 9. Production notes for later stages
- **Peel:** lessons §6 wording only; gentle, close to the skin, never stretching it (the Avatars doc's
  "pulling your skin like this… you're delulu" objection). Skin underneath dewy, not red.
- **U1 forehead lines at rest,** not a brow raise (production log: Kling deepens brow-raise furrows).
- **Patch on = lips closed, no smile** (U1 end, U2 start). The after look comes once the band is off.
- **Expert:** identical voice description in every clip; chroma-green wall for E2–E5, keyed in code; face
  ≥250 px wide; one sentence per clip; cut glitches on frame boundaries. Box and flat lay are code pop-ups
  from real assets; he never holds a product.
- **All video:** no still stands in for any beat; stills are start/end frames only. No slowed clips.
