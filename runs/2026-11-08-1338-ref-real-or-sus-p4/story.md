## 1. Concept
A P4 woman's own night-and-morning clip (patch on, patch off, a softer forehead) is stitched by a calm,
expert-looking stranger. He asks the question she's asking herself ("real or sus?"), explains in plain
words why it works and why this kit beats tape, and calls it: real.

**Why P4:** she wants forehead results "without Botox or filler" and finds every alternative "a mixed bag".
A neutral verdict on a live demo is the help she needs to choose.

- 0–11 s: silent UGC (music and room tone), with the expert reacting silently in the corner.
- 11–29.5 s: the expert talks (native voice).
- 29.5–30.6 s: end card.
