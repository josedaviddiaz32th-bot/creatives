# Prompt review: v4 (hand-light redo + peel clip A)

Reviewer: prompt-reviewer, Nov 8, 2026. I checked these against the owner's two new rules (owner-feedback, last row): (1) hands evenly lit like the face, never darker; (2) peel resistance is smooth and continuous, with no pause. I also checked lessons §6, `learning/peel-recipe.md` and the three stills (S_U1_START, S_U1_END, S_PEEL_MID).

**Verdict: FIX** (all three prompts need fixes before any credits are spent)

| Job | Verdict | What the model would produce, and why that's wrong |
|---|---|---|
| S_U1_START_v2 | FIX | The prompt asks the **lamp** to light the hands. In the reference, that lamp is *behind her on the frame-left side*. Her face is actually lit by soft light from the front. Read literally, the model keeps a backlit, one-sided lamp setup, and that is the exact cause of the dark hands (the owner said: no one-sided lamp light in shots with hands). The front light needs to be named as the source for both her face and her hands, with the lamp kept only as background glow. "A hand's width in front of her face" doesn't give the model a depth it can draw, so the hands can stay big near the lens. Pin the depth with body cues: elbows bent and low, hands at cheek level beside her face, and each hand no bigger than her face is long. Also missing: "one person", and "phone on a stand, nobody holds it" (both her hands are busy). |
| U1_APPLY_v2 | FIX | The prompt says the hand light "never changes", but it never names the light, and it calls the scene "the lamp light", so the model may relight from the lamp behind her as the hands move. Name the same frontal light as the start still. It also never says the hands stay at the same distance from the lens, and hands coming toward the lens is how they went dark and huge in v2. "Brings the band up to her forehead" doesn't say it travels back onto her face from a hand's width out. It's also missing "eyes open except natural blinks". Continuity with the new start (band at eye/brow height, about a hand's width out) and with the end frame (no hands, band on her forehead, head on the pillow) is otherwise fine. |
| PEEL_A | FIX | This breaks peel-recipe rule 1 ("never ask one clip for the whole peel"). The prompt crams the full peel, the release, the carry-out and the look to camera into 4 s, so Kling has to finish everything by 2.8 s. That means the first ~2 s you plan to use run the release line fast, with no slow, even stretch, and the motion is aimed at a release we throw away. Clip A should cover the first half only, spread across the full 4 s, with the pull still going at 4.0 s. Then the handoff frame you pick is mid-motion, never a stop, and that serves the no-pause rule. "Glancing up toward the peeling hand" adds an eye move the still doesn't have (she looks softly ahead), which risks an eye-roll. The phrase "until the frame-left hand leaves, then one" no longer applies. Already OK: injury, talking, slide/smear and doubling guards, frame sides (anchor on frame-left, pull on frame-right, matching the still), even hand light, and no end frame. |

Note for the producer: for PEEL_A, take the last good frame wherever the pull is still smooth and nothing has slid (it may be later than 2 s). Then use that frame as the start for recipe step 3 (clip B).
