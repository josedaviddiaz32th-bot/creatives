## The Botox caption: verdict and safer wording
"When people assume I've had Botox & fillers but I just sleep in my Wrinkle Smooth patches"

This is **not acceptable as written.**

- **What's fine:** it is first-person perception, which is allowed, and "looks like Botox" is close to
  "wrinkles gone".
- **"& fillers":** fillers add volume. That implies a plumping or volumising result, which no line in §1–2
  approves.
- **"assume":** it states that other people routinely mistake her result for injectables. That reads as
  social proof of Botox-equivalence, which is a stronger result claim than §2 approves. It's also the line
  a "can't be real" viewer pushes back on.

**Use:** "When people ask if I've had Botox but I just sleep in my Wrinkle Smooth patches" (15 words). It
keeps the reference's pattern and P4's tension (should I get Botox?). It's a question others ask, not a
stated equivalence, and it drops fillers.

An even safer alternative, if the main session prefers: "When people ask what I've had done… it's just my
Wrinkle Smooth patches".
