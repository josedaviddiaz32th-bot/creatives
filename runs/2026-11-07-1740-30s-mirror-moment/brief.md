## Must avoid

- Banned claim words: "removes", "erases", "reverses", "prevents wrinkles",
  "stops aging", "lifts sagging skin", "stop this process"; permanent results;
  medical or dermatologist claims; invented testimonials, reviews, numbers or
  before/afters; endorsements.
- Botox side-effect or health claims (e.g. the doc's RA/Myasthenia Gravis
  quote) — Reddit opinion, not approved.
- "11s" (say "frown lines" / "forehead lines").
- "8 hours" (say "overnight, 6–8 hours"); "rinse and dry" (say "just clean and
  reuse").
- Smiling with patches on (owner ban); lips closed or small closed-lip smile
  with patches on.
- Mirrors in frame; cream, lotion or shine on the skin with patches; bangs on
  the forehead.
- Patches rendered white, beige, pink, gold, opaque, gel/hydrogel, fabric,
  paper or tape; forehead tab pointing up; under-eye pieces as circles/glasses.
- Static title card as first frame; AI-rendered on-screen text (add in code);
  ALL CAPS in dialogue.
- Hard verbal pivot ("tried everything? Well, I have these patches") — the
  patch arrives inside the story (owner §0). Hook must be a question.
- Any price or deal not re-checked against live Shopify; leaning on the
  guarantee (see caution).
