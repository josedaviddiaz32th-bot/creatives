## 1. Concept

**"Wait, is that me?" bedside confession.** At 36 she opens her front camera,
sees her forehead lines and doesn't recognise herself. She's started dodging
group photos, retinol and creams didn't shift the lines, and Botox isn't for
her. So she adds one more step before bed, and in the morning she looks rested
and "still me", which answers the hook's own question.

**Why it fits her:** the persona doesn't reject her age, she rejects not
recognising herself. She already has a routine (retinol, creams), so the
patches come in as one more step in that routine, not a procedure. That is the
angle's "complement to skincare" and the needle-avoider's "not for me". Belief
moved: **belief 1** (held flat all night, smoother-looking by morning, nothing
under the skin). Objection answered: **"why not just get Botox"**, answered only
with "no needles, nothing under the skin", without knocking Botox.

## 2. Format

**Talking head, split by a silent wearing beat** (`format.md`): night selfie,
bare face, she talks → silent beat, already wearing the full Face Set, she lies
back (lips closed, no smile, no speech, no hands near her face) → next morning,
same bedroom, patches off and lying on their backing sheet on the nightstand,
bare face, she talks. Native Kling voice in clips 1 and 3, with the same voice
description in both. The patch sheet sits on the nightstand from frame one, so
the product is on screen early without a verbal pivot. No mirror is said or
shown.

## 5. The turn, explained

She has just said retinol and creams didn't shift her forehead lines and Botox
isn't for her, so "one more step before bed", with a glance at the sheet that
has sat on her nightstand since frame one, is simply what a woman with a night
routine does next, and it never reads as a pitch.
