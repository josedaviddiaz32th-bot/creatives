# Story — run 2026-11-07-1740 — "30s mirror moment"

## 1. Concept

**"Is that me?" bedside confession.** At 36 she catches her face and doesn't
recognise it, has quietly stopped taking selfies, creams didn't do it and Botox
isn't for her. So she adds one more step before bed, and in the morning she
looks rested and "still me", which answers the hook's own question.

**Why it fits her:** the brief's persona doesn't reject her age, she rejects
not recognising herself. She already has a routine (retinol, creams), so the
patches come in as one more step in that routine, not a procedure. That is the
angle's "complement to skincare" and the needle-avoider's "not for me". Belief
moved: **belief 1** (held flat all night, smoother-looking by morning, nothing
under the skin). Objection answered: **"why not just get Botox"**, answered only
with "no needles, nothing under the skin", without knocking Botox.

## 2. Format

**Talking head, split by a silent routine beat** (`format.md`): night selfie,
bare face, she talks → silent patch-on beat (lips closed, no smile, no speech)
→ next morning, same bedroom, patches already off, bare face, she talks. Native
Kling voice in clips 1 and 3, with the same voice description in both. The
patch sheet sits on the nightstand from frame one, so the product is on screen
early without a verbal pivot. "Mirror" is only said, never shown.

## 3. Hook

**Main (spoken + on-screen):**
> "Ever look in the mirror at thirty-six and think… is that me?"

On-screen text (≤8 words): **"36 and… is that me?"**
Motion in frame one: she is already settling back onto the bed, phone in hand,
with the patch sheet visible on the nightstand.

**Alternative A (photo avoidance, First-Line Fixer):**
> "When did I start hiding in the back of every group photo?"
Text: "When did I start hiding in photos?"

**Alternative B (selfie / filter, her own words):**
> "Why can't I take a selfie without a filter anymore?"
Text: "Selfies without a filter? Not anymore."

Other drafts considered, then dropped: "Am I the only one who stopped taking
selfies at thirty-five?"; "Is it just me, or did my forehead lines show up
overnight?" (it implies a timeframe); "Why do my forehead lines look deeper
every morning?" (that one fits the side-sleeper angle better). All three keepers
are body-agnostic, so the same body can run under each for a hook test.

## 5. The turn, explained

She has just said that creams didn't do it and Botox isn't for her, so "one
more step before bed" is the obvious next thing a woman with a night routine
would do. The sheet has been on her nightstand since frame one, so the patches
show up as part of her bedtime and never as a pitch.
