## Exact fixes

### Fix 1 — Hook (clip 1, 0.0–5.2s): swap the mirror for the front camera, anchor the wrinkle
- **What's wrong:** "Ever look in the mirror at thirty-six and think… is that
  me?" The mirror is never shown (and must not be), so the line and the picture
  don't match. It's generic to any aging persona. The on-screen text "36 and…
  is that me?" doesn't say what she's reacting to, and most people watch sound-off.
- **Rewrite (spoken, 11 words ≈4.8s):**
  > "Ever open your front camera and think… wait, is that me?"
- **Rewrite (on-screen text, ≤8 words, in code):** **"36, forehead lines… is that me?"**
- **Why:** what she says now matches what we see (she *is* on her front
  camera). It hits this persona's trigger (selfies and the camera) and lands
  inside 5s. The text tells a sound-off viewer what the issue is. The "still
  me" payoff still works.
- **Visual note for the storyboarder:** in frame one her brows are slightly
  raised, so her forehead lines read at phone size (subtle; no hand to the face).

### Fix 2 — Body problem (clip 1, 5.2–11.7s): remove the selfie contradiction
- **What's wrong:** "I stopped taking selfies." She says this while filming a
  selfie video. Viewers will notice.
- **Rewrite:**
  > "I dodge group photos now. Retinol, creams… my forehead lines stayed. Botox? Just not for me."
- **Why:** "avoiding group pictures" comes straight from the angle text, it
  doesn't contradict the picture, and "forehead lines" now pays off the hook
  text.

### Fix 3 — Turn (clip 1, 11.7–14.8s): keep it, simplify the action
- Keep: "So, one more step before bed." (or "So now, one more step before bed.")
- Change the note: instead of reaching for the sheet while speaking, she
  **glances toward the nightstand** where the sheet is already in frame. One
  action, no hand-to-object grab during lip sync.

### Fix 4 — Clip 2 (silent): one action, already wearing the full set
- **What's wrong:** "presses the forehead piece on from the centre outward,
  then lies back wearing the full Face Set" is two actions in 4s, and four
  pieces appear that she never applied. This is the "patches already on / apply
  and wear in one shot" failure (§8), plus a hand-to-face action (§8 "hands
  deform").
- **Rewrite:** ~5s. Start frame: sitting on the bed, **already wearing the full
  Face Set** (forehead tab pointing down, two under-eye crescents, two smile-line
  crescents, lips uncovered), calm, lips closed, no smile. Single action: she
  lies back onto the pillow and her eyes close. No hands near her face.
  On-screen text unchanged: "Clean, dry skin. Held flat overnight."
- If the owner wants the application shown, it needs its **own** shot (bare
  start frame → forehead piece fully on end frame) and a budget check. That's
  not required for this story.

### Fix 5 — Clip 3 (morning): name the patches, cut the benefits list, no patches in fingers
- **What's wrong:** "They held my skin flat all night, so it looks smoother.
  Rested, still me. No needles, nothing under the skin. Just clean and reuse."
  "They" has nothing spoken before it. The beat stacks belief 1 + objection +
  belief 4. "So it looks smoother" drops the approved "by morning". "Held in her
  fingers" while talking is a hand-detail risk.
- **Rewrite:**
  > "The patches held my skin flat all night, so by morning it looks smoother. Rested. Still me. No needles, nothing under the skin."
- **CTA (unchanged):** "Link's in my bio if you want to try them."
- **Note:** the patches lie on their backing sheet on the nightstand, in frame.
  Her hands are not holding them. Optional "Next morning" text (in code) in the
  first second of clip 3 so the night → day cut reads instantly. A small smile
  is fine (patches off).
- "Just clean and reuse" is dropped from speech. If wanted, it can go in the
  caption.
