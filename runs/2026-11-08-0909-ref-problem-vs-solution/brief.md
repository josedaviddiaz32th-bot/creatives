## APPROVED CLAIMS: use nothing else (lessons §1–2)
No claim is spoken or shown on screen in this video. The only on-screen words are the reference's three
captions. These govern the visuals and the later posting caption:
1. **Usage:** apply to clean, dry skin with no serum, lotion or oil underneath; press each piece from the
   center outward so it lies flat; wear **overnight (6–8 hours)** (never "8 hours"); peel off gently in the
   morning; **"just clean and reuse"** (never "rinse and dry"); store each piece on its backing film. No
   needles, no tape, no refills, no actives.
2. **Mechanism / belief 1:** holding the skin flat and sealed in moisture all night **visibly smooths lines
   by morning**, with nothing going under the skin.
3. **Opener:** lines pressed in every night while she sleeps are something she can stop ("Some of the lines
   you see in the morning were pressed in overnight." / "It's my pillow, not me.").
4. **Approved result words:** "smoother skin by morning", "softens your lines", "stops the creases you press
   in every night", "wake up looking rested", "removes wrinkles" (never permanent / forever / for good).
5. **What it is:** The Wrinkle Smooth Face Set (5 Pieces) by The Daily Glow Co.: clear, thin (~1.5 mm), soft
   silicone, transparent with a slight frost, never white or opaque; 1 forehead patch (tab pointing DOWN
   between the brows, covers the middle ⅔ of the forehead, not to the hairline) + 4 crescents (2 under the
   eyes, 2 on the smile lines, lips uncovered).
6. **Offer:** 1 Set $45 · 2 Sets $60 (checked live 2026-11-08 in the 2-T run; re-check before any posting
   caption uses a price). This video shows no price.
7. **Guarantee:** stays out (refund page not fixed, lessons §12).

**Claim translation:** her own words "wrinkles"/"aging" stay as her feeling. We never claim the patches stop
aging or prevent wrinkles "forever". Prevention language in the caption stays inside "stops the creases you
press in every night".
