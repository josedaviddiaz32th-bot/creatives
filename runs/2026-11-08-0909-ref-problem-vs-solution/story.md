## 1. Concept
She wakes up at 23 with pillow-crease lines pressed across her face and cries about it. That night her
boyfriend brings The Wrinkle Smooth Face Set, she presses the clear patches on, he tucks her in under a fluffy
throw, and she sleeps cosy and content. **Why it fits P3:** her panic at "wrinkles" in her 20s
("I'm terrified of aging", angle 3's "surprise/reaction") is the Problem. Prevention, by stopping the
creases she presses in every night, is the Solution. The caring-boyfriend beat makes it aspirational and
taggable.
