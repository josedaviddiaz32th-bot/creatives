## 1. Concept
A 36-year-old catches herself on her front camera at night, admits the
forehead lines and the group-photo dodging, says Botox just isn't for her,
realises she doesn't need a needle, tries the patches that night and wakes up
to softer lines. Fits P1: the "is that me?" moment, photo avoidance, tried
retinol, Botox not for her (Avatars angles 1 and 7, Botox-Resistant
Pragmatist). Borrowed mechanism (format.md): contrarian permission from a
peer, product late as her own pick.

## 2. Format
Talking head, front-camera selfie, night → morning. Native voice (she talks
on camera). Three talking clips + one silent action shot.

## 3. Hook
**Main:** "Ever open your front camera and think… wait, is that me?"
On-screen text (code, ≤8 words): **"36, forehead lines… is that me?"**
Visual, frame 1: she lifts the phone and tilts her head slightly toward the lens, relaxed face, faint forehead lines in lamp light; the Face Set sheet sits on the nightstand behind her shoulder.

Alternatives:
- A. "Thirty-six and hiding at the back of every group photo?" (direct callout)
- B. "Why do my forehead lines show up even when I'm not frowning?" (problem question)

Other drafts considered: "Is it just me, or did my forehead lines show up overnight?" · "Retinol, creams… so is Botox really my only option?" · "When did I start looking tired in every photo?"
