2. **Same face in all four shots** (and in 1-M / 1-B later): lock the new P1 character before any video.
3. **Patch state list:** clips 1–2 = 5 pieces on the sheet, none on her; shot 3 = start bare, end with the forehead piece fully on (tab down, ~⅔ of forehead); clip 4 = bare face, forehead piece back on its backing sheet.
4. **Before/after:** night still shows the faint forehead lines clearly; the morning still is an edit of the night still with softer lines, daylight from the right. Don't let the video model airbrush the night clips.
5. **Clear silicone at night** needs lamp side light to read (lessons §8).
6. **Hook text and CTA card** in code, inside the safe zone (below top 14%, above bottom 35%).
