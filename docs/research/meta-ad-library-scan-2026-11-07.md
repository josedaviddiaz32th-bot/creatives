## Patterns worth borrowing (structure only)

- **The tired-mom outcome hook** ("I wake up looking like I slept 12
  hours") is the top-impression TOF/MOF opener in the category: a relatable
  outcome, not a wrinkle callout. Fits P1/P4 and the "tired" angle.
- **Regret hook** ("I wish I'd done my homework before spending a fortune
  at the med spa") speaks to the needle-avoider (P1, P4) at problem-aware.
- **Whitelisting at scale:** one script, many creator faces. Our version
  in organic: one strong concept, several AI faces (iterations).
- **Long-running ads are short and direct** (7–48s, question hook,
  "no injections"); the top-impression ads are long (2–3 min) UGC. Short
  = BOF/MOF; long = the long-form authority/story slot.
- **Proof points competitors use:** retail stockists, Shark Tank, "medical
  grade silicone", "reusable 15–20 times". Ours: whole-face 5-piece kit,
  stays on overnight, just clean and reuse.
- **Gap:** almost nobody leads with the *cause* (lines pressed in
  overnight, side sleeping). Our TOF-U angle is open territory.
