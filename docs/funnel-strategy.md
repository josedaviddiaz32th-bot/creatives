# Funnel strategy: after the click

`docs/creative-strategy.md` covers the videos. This file covers everything a
viewer meets after them: the profile, the pages, the offer, the cart, the
emails. Built from the five funnel videos (`docs/research/funnel-videos.md`)
and the owner's handoff. These are store and email tasks for the owner (or
a later Claude session with Shopify access); the video pipeline doesn't
build them.

**The order matters** (F5): don't build custom funnels before we know which
persona wins. Phase 1 is cheap and makes the test readable; phase 2 is built
only for winners.

## The five decisions she makes

Every piece below exists to win one of these (F3).

| # | Her question | Where it's decided | What wins it |
|---|--------------|--------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Do I trust this brand enough to click? | The video, then the profile | Real-looking videos; a profile that works as a landing page; later, creator partnership ads |
| 2 | Is this for me? | The first screen after the click | A page that continues the video's exact problem; an honest quiz that routes her to the right set |
| 3 | Buy now or later? | Product page, cart, checkout | A clear offer with a real reason to act now, proof, risk reversal; email/SMS capture for the ones who wait |
| 4 | Was that a good decision? | The days after purchase | A personal thank-you, how-to-apply help before the parcel arrives, shipping updates, a check-in |
| 5 | Should I buy again? | Weeks later | New areas to treat, gifts, a replacement set, word of mouth |

## Phase 1: the test week (cheap, do now)

1. **Profile as a landing page (decision 1).** Pin three posts on TikTok and
   Instagram: how it works (30s), a before/after, "does it stay on all
   night?". Bio: one line on what it is plus the link.
2. **Comment keywords (decisions 1–3).** Every video asks viewers to comment
   its keyword (creative strategy section 4). Reply with the link by DM
   (by hand this week, or an automation tool later) and add a UTM per
   keyword (`?utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=PILLOW`) so store sessions
   trace back to the video.
3. **Email/SMS popup (decision 3).** On the product page, a few seconds
   after landing, with one question: "Which lines bother you most?
   Forehead · under-eyes · smile lines · chest". The answer tags her for the
   welcome emails. Offer: the free "Healthy Skin After 30" guide (today it's
   only sent after purchase) or the current deal.
4. **Two welcome emails (decision 3).** Immediately: the guide plus how the
   patches work for *her* area. Next day: the before/after, the offer, the
   main objection for her area (fall-off for forehead, gentleness for
   under-eyes).
5. **Product page basics (decisions 2–3).**
   - Lead with the strongest promise in approved words ("removes your lines
     overnight; just clean and reuse").
   - Show the bundle per unit: "2 sets, $30 each".
   - Real deadline only: the Black Friday "2nd set $15, ends Dec 1".
   - A large FAQ built from the Reddit objections: falls off, side
     sleepers, irritation, moisturiser underneath, how long results last,
     how to clean, shipping time.
   - **Fix the refund page** (it contradicts the 30-day guarantee, lessons
     §12). The guarantee is the strongest risk reversal we have, and it's
     unusable until then.
6. **Health check.** AOV × conversion rate (as a number) should reach 250
   (F4). At ~$55 AOV that's ~4.5% conversion. Track revenue per visitor.

## Phase 2: for winning personas (after the test week)

Build only for the 1–2 personas that win, matched to how their winning
videos talk (F3, F4, F5).

| Traffic from | Page | Built like |
|--------------|------|------------|
| TOF (unaware) | **Advertorial** | Problem she just saw → the cause she didn't know (pressed in overnight, expression lines setting) → why creams and Botox don't fit → the overnight patch → proof → offer |
| MOF (problem/solution-aware) | **Listicle** | "7 reasons women who skip Botox switch to overnight patches" / "5 things side sleepers should know about wrinkle patches" |
| BOF (product-aware) | **Product page** with the persona's offer | Same visuals and words as her videos |

**Offer per persona** (F5: the offer is part of the congruent funnel):

| Persona | Offer idea (owner sets prices) |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| P1 forehead, skips Botox | Face Set ×2 (the core bundle) |
| P2 side sleeper | Face Set + Chest Pad ("the lines your pillow makes") |
| P3 20s, prevention | Face Set, single, entry price |
| P4 under-eyes | Face Set + Eye Patches |
| E1 weight loss | Face, Neck & Chest Set (11 pieces) |

**Quiz (decision 2).** "Which lines bother you most, and how do you sleep?"
routes honestly to the Face Set, Eye Patches, Smile Line, Chest Pad or
the 11-piece set, and captures the email before the result.

## Order value: cart and after purchase (F1, F4)

- **Cart:** one-click add-ons that fit her area: Eye Patches, Chest Pad,
  Hand Patches, a second set.
- **Checkout:** shipping protection (useful with 10–14 day delivery).
- **Thank-you page, one-time offer:** solve the next problem the first one
  creates (F1): "Your forehead's handled overnight. Your under-eyes and
  smile lines show more now" → the add-on at a one-time price.
- Upsell prices step down after the first one.

## After purchase (decisions 4–5)

| When | Message | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| Right after | Personal thank-you from the founder: the story, "reply with any question" | Cuts remorse |
| Day 1–3 | **How to apply so it stays on all night** (clean dry skin, nothing underneath, press from the center out), as a 30s video | Fall-off is the category's #1 complaint (lessons §2); this prevents bad reviews and refunds |
| During shipping | Tracking updates with a tip each | 10–14 days is long; silence breeds doubt |
| Day 7 after delivery | "How are your first nights?" + cleaning and storage | Use = results = repeat purchase |
| ~Day 21 | Her next area (eyes, smile lines, chest), gift idea ("one for your mom") | Repeat purchase for a reusable product |
| When a set wears out (owner: how many uses?) | Replacement reminder | Natural reorder |
| Repeat buyers | VIP segment: early access, input on new products | Loyalty and word of mouth |

Learn the real reorder timing and what people buy second from Shopify data,
then retime these (F4).

## What not to copy from the funnel videos

- Subscriptions: they don't fit a reusable product.
- Fake urgency (resetting timers, invented low stock) and quizzes that say
  "perfect fit" whatever you answer: they're easy to call out in comments,
  and comments are part of what we measure.
- Multi-page upsell chains before we know the basics convert.
