# Creative strategy: Wrinkle Smooth Patches

Every video starts here. Stage 1 (`persona-picker`) reads this file and
`runs/history.json`, picks the next open slot in the test plan, and every
later stage builds that slot. A video without a slot doesn't get made.

Sources: the owner's instructions (Nov 7, 2026), `docs/lessons-learned.md`,
the Avatars doc, `docs/research/creative-strategy-web-research.md` and
`docs/research/creator-videos.md` (where the contradictions are settled).

## 1. Why this exists

Paid Meta ads didn't work for the owner. Before spending again, we run a
**5–6 day organic test** on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook: ~3 videos a day,
built exactly as if they were Meta ads, posted as regular posts. The goal is
to learn **which persona × awareness level × format** combinations hold
attention and send people to the store. Winners get iterated in week 2 and
become the first paid ads.

## 2. How the pieces fit together

Each video is one cell in this stack. Fill every row before writing a word.

| Layer | Question | Values for us |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| **Persona** | Who is she? | P1–P4 below (from the Avatars doc) |
| **Desire** | What does she want, underneath? | Being liked / attraction (photos, partner) · comfort (sleep, no needles) · safety (no side effects) · winning (in control of her body) |
| **Awareness** | What does she already know? | Unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware → most aware (Schwartz) |
| **Funnel stage** | What's this video's job? | TOF = make her feel seen · MOF = make patches the believable fix · BOF = remove the last objection, get the click |
| **Belief** | Which ONE belief does it move? | Lessons §2, beliefs 1–5 |
| **Format** | How is it delivered? | Section 5 catalog |
| **Hook** | First 2–5s | A question that also calls her out (section 6) |

**Angle = persona + desire + awareness.** Two videos with the same angle are
the same concept, even with a different hook (creator video 1, Meta's
"similar ads count as one"). A new hook on the same video is a *hook test*,
not a new concept.

### Awareness → funnel → what the video does

| Funnel | Awareness | Her state | Video job | Belief (lessons §2) | Product appears | Length | Primary metric |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|--------|----------------|
| **TOF** | Unaware, problem-aware | Noticed the lines, doesn't know what to do, or hasn't named it yet | Name her exact moment so she thinks "that's me". Story, open loops. | Optional opener ("some lines are pressed in overnight") or belief 1 at the end | Second half only. It can sit in frame (nightstand) without comment. | 35–60s | Hook rate, avg watch %, shares, follows |
| **MOF** | Solution-aware | Knows creams, Botox, tape and patches exist; doubts patches | Make patches the *right kind* of fix: mechanism, demo, before/after, why others failed | 1 (mechanism) or 2 (others fell off) or 3 (gentle) | Early; it's the subject | 25–40s | Hold rate / completion, saves, profile visits, comments |
| **BOF** | Product-aware, most aware | Knows our patches, hasn't bought | Answer ONE objection and give the offer | 4 (value) or 5 (guarantee, **blocked until the refund page is fixed**, lessons §12) | First second | 15–25s | Bio-link clicks, Shopify sessions, orders |

### How one persona moves down the funnel (organic version)

Paid ads move people with retargeting. Organic can't, so the chain is held
together by:

1. **The same face per persona.** P1 is always the same character in all
   three of her videos (Higgsfield Element/reference), so a viewer who saw
   her TOF video recognises her in the MOF one.
2. **"Part 2" continuity.** TOF ends on an open loop ("what finally worked
   is in part 2"); MOF opens as the follow-up; BOF is the "you asked, here's
   the link" video.
3. **Posting order.** A persona's TOF goes out before her MOF, and MOF before
   BOF (section 7).
4. **The profile.** Pin each persona's TOF video once posted; the bio link
   is the BOF destination.

Talking-head confession · authority / expert explainer · night-to-morning · GRWM / night routine · demo /

## 6. Script rules that come from the research

- **Hook = a question that calls her out.** It names her age, moment or body
  area, so the wrong people scroll past (creator video 2) and the platform
  learns who it's for. Visual, sound and ≤8-word on-screen text all land in
  the first 2s (lessons §3).
- **Open loops** in anything over ~25s: after the first body beat, a line
  like "but that wasn't the weird part" keeps her watching (creator video 6).
- **Product timing follows the stage** (section 2 table). TOF never mentions
  the product in the first half.
- **Peer or authority, both allowed.** A peer who looks like the persona,
  or an expert-style presenter who teaches (creator video 5). Test both. The
  only limit: no stated credential she doesn't have ("I'm a dermatologist").
- **One belief, one objection, one CTA** per video.
- **CTA by stage:** TOF "part 2 tomorrow" / follow; MOF "they're in my bio";
  BOF the offer, subtly.

## 8. Reading results and iterating

The `results-analyst` does this every morning and keeps
`learning/playbook.md` and `learning/next-moves.md` current; this section
is the method it follows.

**Tag every video** in `runs/history.json` (one line per run):
`slot, persona, desire, awareness, funnel, format, hook_type, belief,
objection, seconds, credits, platform links`, then the metrics once read.

**Metrics** (per platform, read 48h after posting):
- Hook rate: TikTok 2s views ÷ views; Instagram/Facebook 3s views ÷ views.
- Hold: average watch % and completion rate.
- Shares, saves, comments, follows, profile visits.
- Bio-link clicks and Shopify sessions/orders by day and platform.
- Audience age and gender: did it reach the persona we aimed at?

Benchmarks conflict, so **this week is the baseline**. Organic numbers on a
new account are small and noisy: only call a winner when it's clearly ahead
of its own stage's median (≈1.5× or more) on that stage's primary metric
(section 2), not on views.

**Diagnosis** (lessons §11): weak hook rate → new opening; strong hook,
collapsing hold → the body didn't deliver what the hook promised; watched
but no clicks → change the reason, offer or CTA; clicks but no orders → it's
the page or offer, not the creative.

**Iterations (days 5–6):** take the top 2–3 videos and remake each one with
the **same angle in a different format, face or setting**. A pure hook swap
is allowed only as a labelled hook test, at most 2 in the week, and never
counts as a new concept.

## 9. Budget

Kling 3.0 Pro with native sound ≈ 2.5 credits/s, plus ~5–10 for stills.
Rough cost by stage: BOF ≈ 45–70, MOF ≈ 70–105, TOF ≈ 95–155 credits.

| | Videos | Est. credits |
|--|--------|--------------|
| 12 concept slots | 4 TOF + 4 MOF + 4 BOF | ≈ 1,050–1,250 |
| 6 iterations | mixed | ≈ 450–550 |
| **Week** | 18 | **≈ 1,500–1,800** |

The balance was 1,200 at the last check, so the full week needs more
credits, or Kling Standard (≈2 credits/s) for TOF, or fewer iterations.
Per-video caps by stage live in `pipeline/config.yaml`.
