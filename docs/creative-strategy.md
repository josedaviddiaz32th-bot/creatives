# Creative strategy: Wrinkle Smooth Patches

Every video starts here. Stage 1 (`persona-picker`) reads this file and
`runs/history.json`, picks the next open slot in the test plan, and every
later stage builds that slot. A video without a slot doesn't get made. What
happens after the click (pages, offers, email) is in
`docs/funnel-strategy.md`.

Sources: the owner's instructions (Nov 7, 2026), `docs/lessons-learned.md`,
the Avatars doc, and `docs/research/` (web research, the creator videos, the
funnel videos).

## 1. Why this exists

Paid Meta ads didn't work for the owner. Before spending again, we run a
**5–6 day organic test** on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook: 3 videos a day,
built exactly as if they were Meta ads, posted as regular posts. The goal is
to learn **which persona × awareness level × video type** combinations hold
attention and create buying intent. Winners get iterated and, later, become
the first paid ads and the first custom landing pages.

## 2. The funnel: what each stage's videos must do

Most of the market isn't ready to buy. Only a few people already know
patches and want them; most have never connected their lines (or looking
tired or angry in photos) to anything they can fix. A funnel that only talks
to buyers only reaches the few. So **every stage gets its own videos with
their own job**, and most videos go to the top and middle (research and
funnel videos F1, F4, F5).

**Every video stands alone** (owner): one video starts an idea and ends it.
No "part 2", no sequels, no cliffhanger into another video. Open loops close
inside the same video.

### The three stages

| | **TOF** | **MOF** | **BOF** |
|--|---------|---------|---------|
| **Awareness** | Unaware | Problem-aware (MOF-P) and solution-aware (MOF-S) | Product-aware and most aware |
| **What she knows** | She sees lines or looks tired or angry in photos, but doesn't know why, or doesn't see it as something she can fix | MOF-P: knows her lines bother her, not what fixes them. MOF-S: knows creams, Botox, tape and patches; doubts patches | Knows our patches; something stops her from buying |
| **The video's job** | **The problem itself:** show it, reveal a cause she didn't know, make her feel it | MOF-P: why what she tried doesn't fit, and that an overnight patch is a different kind of fix. MOF-S: make *these* patches believable | Remove ONE objection, make the offer, give a real reason to act now |
| **Product** | None, or one closing line or shot as the answer | MOF-P: arrives as the answer, mid-video. MOF-S: the subject from early on | From the first second |
| **Proof** | The insight itself; visuals of the problem (pillow crease, morning lines, photo moment) | Mechanism, demo, before/after, side-by-side, stays-on test | Before/after, specs, price math, offer, guarantee (once the refund page is fixed) |
| **Belief (lessons §2)** | Optional opener: "some lines are pressed in overnight" | 1 (overnight smoothing), 2 (others fell off), 3 (gentle) | 3, 4 (value), 5 (guarantee) |
| **Length** (`video.seconds_by_stage`) | 30–50s | 25–45s | 15–25s |
| **CTA** | Soft or none: "comment PILLOW and I'll send you what I use" | "Comment STAY and I'll send you the link" / link in bio | The offer, subtly: "2 sets are $60 right now, link's in my bio" |
| **Primary metric** | Hook rate, avg watch %, shares, saves, follows; reached the persona's age/gender | Completion, saves, profile visits, comments, keyword comments | Keyword comments, link clicks, store sessions, orders, revenue per visitor |
| **Where the click goes, test week** | Product page | Product page | Product page |
| **Where it goes once a persona wins** (`docs/funnel-strategy.md`) | Advertorial: problem → cause → solution | Listicle: "7 reasons…" | Product page with the persona's offer |
| **Share of videos** | ~40% | ~35% | ~25% |

### How the stages connect without sequels

1. **Same face per persona.** P1 is the same character in all her videos, so
   someone who saw her TOF video recognises her in the MOF one.
2. **Same problem language.** The cause a TOF video reveals is the one the
   MOF video fixes and the BOF video sells.
3. **The profile is a landing page.** Everyone who likes a video checks the
   profile. Pin three posts: how it works, a before/after, "does it stay on
   all night?" (F3).
4. **A comment keyword per video.** "Comment PILLOW and I'll send you the
   link" turns interest into a conversation and tells us which video created
   intent (the single bio link can't). Each slot has its own keyword
   (section 4).
5. **Email capture on the site** catches the people who aren't ready yet
   (`docs/funnel-strategy.md`).
6. **Later, in paid:** retargeting does the connecting. People who watched
   50% of a TOF video see MOF videos; MOF engagers and site visitors see BOF.

### Building blocks of every video

| Layer | Values |
|-------|--------|
| Persona | P1–P4, plus explore avatars (section 3) |
| Desire | Being liked / attraction (photos, partner) · comfort (sleep, no needles) · safety (no side effects) · winning (control over her body) |
| Awareness and stage | Section 2 table |
| Belief and objection | Lessons §2 |
| Video type | Section 5, by stage |
| Presenter | Peer who looks like the persona · authority look (no stated credential) · voiceover only |
| Copy angle | Personal narrative · evidence (demo, before/after, mechanism) · direct response (problem, numbers, offer) (F2) |
| Hook | A question that calls her out (section 6) |

**Angle = persona + desire + awareness.** Same angle = same concept, even
with a new hook. A hook swap is a labelled *hook test*, not a new concept
(Meta: similar ads count as one).

## 5. Video types by stage

Adapted from winning ads the creative-scout breaks down (any category; see
`learning/swipe-file/`). Copy the shape, never the words, face or brand.

- **TOF (unaware):** curiosity explainer ("why your lines are deepest at
  7am") · POV / skit of the moment she notices · authority-look explainer ·
  green-screen reaction to a post or article · counterintuitive fact /
  myth · "things your face does while you sleep" · voiceover over B-roll ·
  talking-head confession.
- **MOF (problem- and solution-aware):** "I tried everything" story ·
  night routine / GRWM · mechanism explainer · us vs them · demo · stays-on
  test · before/after (any form) · myth-busting · long-form authority video.
- **BOF (product- and most aware):** objection answer · "things I wish I
  knew" · price math · unboxing / first reaction · rapid-fire FAQ ·
  before/after + offer · near-static offer card with a real deadline.

## 6. Script rules

- **Hook = a question that calls her out**: her age, moment or body area, so
  the wrong people scroll past and the platform learns who it's for. Visual,
  sound and ≤8-word on-screen text land in the first 2s (lessons §3).
- **TOF is about the problem.** The product is at most the answer in the
  last seconds. Don't name the product category in the hook.
- **Open loops** in anything over ~25s, closed inside the same video.
- **Peer or authority, both allowed.** The authority look is fine; a stated
  credential she doesn't have is not.
- **One belief, one objection, one CTA** per video.
- **The keyword CTA** uses the slot's keyword, said naturally ("comment
  PILLOW and I'll send you what I use"), and appears in the caption.
- **BOF urgency is real or absent:** a real end date (e.g. the Black Friday
  offer's), never a fake timer or fake low stock.

## 8. Reading results and iterating

The `results-analyst` does this every morning and keeps
`learning/playbook.md` and `learning/next-moves.md` current; this section
is the method it follows.

**Tag every video** in `runs/history.json`: `slot, persona, desire,
awareness, funnel, video type, presenter, copy angle, hook_type, belief,
objection, keyword, seconds, credits, platform links`, then the metrics.

**Metrics** (per platform, 48h after posting):
- Hook rate: TikTok 2s views ÷ views; Instagram/Facebook 3s views ÷ views.
- Hold: average watch % and completion.
- Shares, saves, comments, follows, profile visits.
- **Keyword comments and DMs** for the video's keyword.
- Bio-link clicks; store sessions, orders, email sign-ups by day.
- **Revenue per visitor** (order value × conversion rate) once store data is
  connected.
- Audience age and gender: did it reach the persona?

Each video is judged against **its own stage's median** on that stage's
primary metric (section 2), never on views alone. This week is the baseline;
organic numbers are small, so only clear gaps (≈1.5×) count.

**Diagnosis** (lessons §11): weak hook rate → the opening; strong hook,
weak hold → the body didn't deliver what the hook promised; watched but no
keyword comments or clicks → the reason, offer or CTA; clicks but no orders
→ the page or offer, not the video.

**Iterations:** the top videos get remade with the **same angle in a
different video type, face or setting**. A hook swap is only a labelled hook
test (max 2 a week).

## 9. Budget

Kling 3.0 Pro with native sound ≈ 2.5 credits/s, plus stills. Rough cost:
TOF ≈ 85–135, MOF ≈ 70–120, BOF ≈ 45–70 credits. Voiceover videos (E1-T)
are cheaper because silent B-roll runs with `sound: off`.

| | Videos | Est. credits |
|--|--------|--------------|
| 14 concepts | 6 TOF + 4 MOF + 4 BOF | ≈ 1,200–1,350 |
| 4 iterations | mixed | ≈ 300–400 |
| **Week** | 18 | **≈ 1,500–1,750** |

The balance was 1,200 at the last check: enough for days 1–4. Days 5–6
need more credits, or fewer iterations. Caps per stage live in
`pipeline/config.yaml`.

## 10. After the test week

1. **Exploit / explore:** ~70% of new videos deepen winning personas and
   angles in new video types; ~30% test new avatars, angles and types
   (`learning.explore_share`).
2. **Build the custom funnel for winners only** (F5): for the 1–2 personas
   that win, an advertorial for their TOF traffic, a listicle for MOF, and a
   persona-matched offer on the product page (`docs/funnel-strategy.md`).
3. **Paid:** winners become the first ads, grouped by persona; launch new
   creative weekly; retarget by video views and site visits (section 2).
4. **Macro moves next:** new avatars (Spanish-language buyers, the explore
   avatars that won), then new offers and hero products (Eye Patches, Chest
   Pad, the 11-piece set).
